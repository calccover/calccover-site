About Calccover

Calccover builds free commercial insurance calculators for small business owners, contractors, and fleet operators.

Why We Built This

Commercial insurance premiums are one of the biggest unknown costs when starting or running a business. Brokers quote prices that vary wildly, and there's no easy way to know if a quote is reasonable. We built Calccover to give business owners a realistic estimate before they ever pick up the phone.

How Our Calculators Work

Each calculator uses industry-standard rating factors drawn from publicly available insurance data, state filings, and industry averages. You input a few key variables — revenue, payroll, industry type, location — and the calculator estimates a realistic premium range.

What Our Results Mean

Our results are estimates, not quotes. Actual premiums depend on your specific business operations, claims history, credit profile, and the underwriting standards of each insurer. Use our calculators as a starting point for conversations with licensed brokers, not as a substitute for a formal quote.

Independence

Calccover is an independent resource. We are not owned by, affiliated with, or endorsed by any insurance carrier or broker. We may earn a commission if you request a quote through a partner link, at no cost to you. This never affects the accuracy of our calculators.
