Best BOP Insurance for Small Business

A Business Owner's Policy (BOP) bundles general liability and commercial property into one policy at a 10–25% discount. Here are the best carriers for BOP coverage in 2026.

By Emiliano · Last updated 2026-10-15 · 10 min read

#1

Hiscox

Best for: Small businesses in low-risk industries needing fast online quotes

Coverage: General Liability, Professional Liability / E&O, BOP, Cyber Liability

Typical GL cost: $40–$100/month
Typical BOP cost: $60–$180/month

AM Best rating: A (Excellent)

Notable: Known for fast online quoting (as little as 5 minutes) and low minimum premiums

#2

Next Insurance

Best for: Small businesses and independent contractors wanting instant online purchase

Coverage: General Liability, Professional Liability, Commercial Auto, Workers' Comp, BOP

Typical GL cost: $25–$80/month
Typical BOP cost: $50–$150/month

AM Best rating: A- (Excellent)

Notable: Digital-first, fully online purchase with same-day certificate of insurance

#3

The Hartford

Best for: Businesses with employees needing comprehensive coverage including workers' comp

Coverage: General Liability, BOP, Workers' Comp, Commercial Auto, Cyber

Typical GL cost: $60–$150/month
Typical BOP cost: $80–$250/month

AM Best rating: A+ (Superior)

Notable: One of the largest US commercial carriers — strong for workers' comp and multi-state operations

#4

Nationwide

Best for: Businesses wanting bundled business + personal insurance

Coverage: General Liability, BOP, Workers' Comp, Commercial Auto, Cyber

Typical GL cost: $60–$150/month
Typical BOP cost: $80–$250/month

AM Best rating: A+ (Superior)

Notable: Strong bundle discounts if you also have personal insurance (auto, home) with Nationwide

#5

CoverWallet

Best for: Small businesses wanting broker service with digital experience

Coverage: General Liability, Workers' Comp, Commercial Auto, BOP, Cyber, E&O

Typical GL cost: $50–$120/month
Typical BOP cost: $70–$200/month

AM Best rating: A (Excellent) — backed by Aon

Notable: Digital broker backed by Aon — hybrid of online quoting and human advisor support

Which carrier should you choose?

For fast online purchase: Hiscox or Next Insurance. For comprehensive coverage with workers' comp: The Hartford. For bundling with personal insurance: Nationwide. For broker guidance: CoverWallet.

Rates for identical coverage vary 20–30% between carriers. Get quotes from at least 3 before deciding.
