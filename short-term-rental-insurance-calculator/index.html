How Much Does Short-Term Rental Insurance Cost?

Short-term rental insurance policies for platforms like Airbnb and VRBO typically range from 1.5% to 3% of your gross annual rental revenue, translated into an annual premium. For a property generating $60,000 annually, you can expect to pay anywhere from $900 to $1,800 per year depending on your geographical zone, property valuation, and structural characteristics.

Standard homeowner policies almost never cover commercial short-term rental activities. Relying solely on host protection guarantees from booking platforms leaves significant coverage gaps, especially regarding liability limits and sustained business interruption losses during unexpected property damage repairs.

What Factors Affect Your Premium?

Location is one of the most critical drivers of commercial insurance pricing. Properties located in coastal storm zones, wildfire-prone regions, or dense urban centers experience higher base premiums due to elevated frequency and severity risks. Mountain chalets and ski-in properties also carry specialized winter hazard adjustments.

Property type and valuation directly impact the rebuilding cost calculation. A luxury estate with high-end finishes and extensive square footage requires higher structural coverage limits than a standard suburban condominium. Additionally, properties with amenities like swimming pools, hot tubs, or wood-burning stoves incur higher general liability endorsements to mitigate slip-and-fall or fire exposure claims.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does standard homeowners insurance cover Airbnb rentals?

No. Standard homeowners insurance policies explicitly exclude business activities and commercial hosting. If a guest is injured or property is damaged while paying to stay at your home, a standard insurer will likely deny the claim.

What does business interruption insurance cover for short-term rentals?

Lost rental income protection covers the revenue you would have earned if a covered peril—such as a fire or severe storm—renders the property uninhabitable, ensuring your mortgage and fixed operating expenses are paid during repairs.

Are platform guarantees enough protection?

Host damage and liability guarantees provided by booking websites are helpful, but they often have strict exclusions, lengthy claim approval processes, and high deductibles. Dedicated commercial short-term rental policies provide primary, comprehensive protection without relying on third-party dispute resolutions.
