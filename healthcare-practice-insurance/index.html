Healthcare Practice Insurance: Complete 2026 Guide

Healthcare practices face the highest liability exposure of any small business category. This hub covers insurance for medical offices, dental practices, and childcare centers.

By Emiliano · Last updated 2026-10-16 · 10 min read

$2,500 – $15,000
Typical annual cost for a small healthcare practice

What insurance does a healthcare practice insurance business need?

Most healthcare practice insurance businesses need a stack of coverages. The priority list:

  • Professional Liability / Malpractice — The core coverage for healthcare practices. Covers claims that your treatment, diagnosis, or care caused patient harm.
  • General Liability — Slip-and-falls at your office, third-party property damage, and non-medical injuries.
  • Commercial Property — Medical equipment, exam tables, furniture, tenant improvements. Medical equipment is expensive to replace.
  • Workers' Compensation — Employee injuries (needlesticks, lifting injuries, slips). Mandatory with W-2 employees.
  • Cyber Liability — HIPAA-covered entities must protect patient data. Cyber coverage is essential — HIPAA fines alone can exceed $1M.
  • Commercial Umbrella — Excess liability. Recommended for any practice with high-revenue procedures or multiple providers.

Frequently asked questions

How much does medical office insurance cost?

Medical office insurance costs $4,000–$12,000 per year for a small practice. Professional liability (malpractice) is the largest cost, running $3,000–$8,000 per provider. GL, property, and workers' comp add another $1,500–$4,000.

Do medical offices need malpractice insurance?

Yes. Malpractice (professional liability) insurance is essential for any healthcare provider. Most state licensing boards require proof of coverage for physicians, nurses, and mid-level providers. Hospital privileges and insurance panel contracts also require it.

How much does daycare insurance cost?

Daycare insurance costs $2,000–$9,000 per year depending on size, number of children, and state. General liability, professional liability for child injuries, and property coverage are standard. States often require minimum coverage limits for licensing.

Do dental offices need cyber liability insurance?

Yes. Dental offices store patient health records and take card payments — both are HIPAA-regulated and ransomware targets. A basic cyber policy costs $1,000–$3,000 per year and is often required by insurance panels and state boards.

Do home daycares need commercial insurance?

Yes. Homeowners policies exclude business use for childcare. You need a home-based business endorsement or a standalone daycare policy. Most states require minimum coverage for licensing.

Can I use my personal health insurance for my practice?

No. Personal health insurance covers you as an individual — it does not cover claims against your practice. Professional liability, GL, property, and workers' comp are all separate commercial coverages.
