Frequently asked questions

Is Hiscox or Next Insurance cheaper for small business insurance?

Hiscox and Next Insurance are similar in pricing. Hiscox runs $40–$100/month, while Next Insurance runs $25–$80/month. For identical coverage, rates vary 20–30% depending on your class code, revenue, and state — so the cheapest option depends on your specific business.

Which is better for general liability insurance, Hiscox or Next Insurance?

Both Hiscox and Next Insurance offer general liability. Hiscox is best for small businesses in low-risk industries needing fast online quotes. Next Insurance is best for small businesses and independent contractors wanting instant online purchase.

Does Hiscox or Next Insurance offer workers' comp?

Next Insurance offers workers' comp. Hiscox does not write workers' comp directly — you would need a different carrier for that coverage.

Can I switch from Hiscox to Next Insurance mid-year?

Yes. Commercial insurance policies can be cancelled and replaced at any time. If you paid annually, the old carrier refunds unused premium (pro-rata or short-rate). The main caution is workers' comp — mid-year changes can complicate your experience modification calculation because the policy period does not align with the standard experience period.
