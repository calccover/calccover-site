CoverWallet vs Hiscox: Which Is Better for Small Business?

Both CoverWallet and Hiscox are top-rated commercial insurance carriers for small business. The right choice depends on your industry, revenue, and how you prefer to buy coverage. Here's a side-by-side comparison to help you decide.

Frequently asked questions

Is CoverWallet or Hiscox cheaper for small business insurance?

CoverWallet and Hiscox are similar in pricing. CoverWallet runs $50–$120/month, while Hiscox runs $40–$100/month. For identical coverage, rates vary 20–30% depending on your class code, revenue, and state — so the cheapest option depends on your specific business.

Which is better for general liability insurance, CoverWallet or Hiscox?

Both CoverWallet and Hiscox offer general liability. CoverWallet is best for small businesses wanting broker service with digital experience. Hiscox is best for small businesses in low-risk industries needing fast online quotes.

Does CoverWallet or Hiscox offer workers' comp?

CoverWallet offers workers' comp. Hiscox does not write workers' comp directly — you would need a different carrier for that coverage.

Can I switch from CoverWallet to Hiscox mid-year?

Yes. Commercial insurance policies can be cancelled and replaced at any time. If you paid annually, the old carrier refunds unused premium (pro-rata or short-rate). The main caution is workers' comp — mid-year changes can complicate your experience modification calculation because the policy period does not align with the standard experience period.
