Frequently asked questions

Is Next Insurance or Thimble cheaper for small business insurance?

Next Insurance and Thimble are similar in pricing. Next Insurance runs $25–$80/month, while Thimble runs $5/day or $50/month. For identical coverage, rates vary 20–30% depending on your class code, revenue, and state — so the cheapest option depends on your specific business.

Which is better for general liability insurance, Next Insurance or Thimble?

Both Next Insurance and Thimble offer general liability. Next Insurance is best for small businesses and independent contractors wanting instant online purchase. Thimble is best for gig workers, freelancers, and short-term or event-based coverage.

Does Next Insurance or Thimble offer workers' comp?

Next Insurance offers workers' comp. Thimble does not write workers' comp directly — you would need a different carrier for that coverage.

Can I switch from Next Insurance to Thimble mid-year?

Yes. Commercial insurance policies can be cancelled and replaced at any time. If you paid annually, the old carrier refunds unused premium (pro-rata or short-rate). The main caution is workers' comp — mid-year changes can complicate your experience modification calculation because the policy period does not align with the standard experience period.
