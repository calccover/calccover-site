Professional Services Insurance: Complete 2026 Guide

Professional services businesses have different insurance needs than physical trades. Errors & omissions, cyber liability, and professional liability are the core coverages. This hub covers what consultants, IT professionals, and service providers need.

By Emiliano · Last updated 2026-10-16 · 10 min read

What insurance does a professional services insurance business need?

Most professional services insurance businesses need a stack of coverages. The priority list:

  • Professional Liability / E&O — Claims that your advice, design, or work product caused client financial loss. The core coverage for most professional services businesses.
  • General Liability — Third-party bodily injury and property damage. Required for client visits and any physical office space.
  • Cyber Liability — Data breaches, ransomware, and business email compromise. Essential if you handle client data or take card payments.
  • Home-Based Business Endorsement — Covers business property and liability if you work from home. Homeowners policies exclude business use.
  • Commercial Umbrella — Excess liability protection. Cheap ($200–$800/year for $1M) and recommended for anyone with $1M+ revenue.
  • Employment Practices Liability (EPLI) — Wrongful termination, discrimination, harassment claims. Recommended once you have 5+ employees.

Frequently asked questions

How much does professional services insurance cost?

Professional services insurance costs $1,500–$6,000 per year for most small firms. A solo consultant pays $1,500–$4,000; a 5-person firm pays $3,000–$6,000+. The biggest cost drivers are E&O limits, revenue, and cyber exposure.

Do consultants need errors & omissions insurance?

Yes, if you provide advice or recommendations. E&O covers claims that your advice caused a client financial loss — even if you did nothing wrong. Most client contracts require proof of $1M E&O coverage.

What is the difference between E&O and professional liability?

They're the same thing. Errors & Omissions (E&O) and Professional Liability are interchangeable terms for the same coverage type. Both cover claims arising from your professional services.

Do IT consultants need cyber liability insurance?

Yes. IT consultants are prime targets for cyber claims because you handle client systems and data. A basic cyber policy costs $500–$3,000 per year and covers breach notification, legal defense, and regulatory fines.

Is professional liability insurance required by law?

Not for most professions. But many state licensing boards (engineers, architects, CPAs) require proof of E&O. Client contracts often require it as well. Most B2B clients won't sign without evidence of $1M E&O coverage.

Can I use my homeowners policy for a home-based consulting business?

No. Homeowners policies exclude all business liability and cap business property coverage at $2,500 in most cases. Even a solo consultant working from home needs a home-based business endorsement or standalone BOP.
